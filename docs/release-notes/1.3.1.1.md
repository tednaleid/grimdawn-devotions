# Site updated for Grim Dawn 1.3.1.1

All pages of [the site](https://tednaleid.github.io/grimdawn-devotions/) now use game data from
v1.3.1.1 (Steam build 25813250).

## Devotion planner

Pet resistances were removed from devotions as part of the patch's pet resist rework:

- Bysmiel's Bonds: Vitality
- Crane: Poison & Acid, Vitality
- Empty Throne: Pierce, Aether, Chaos
- Ishtak, the Spring Maiden: Poison & Acid, Bleeding
- Korvaak, the Eldritch Sun: Chaos
- Nighttalon: Elemental
- Rhowan's Crown: Elemental
- Shepherd's Crook: Elemental
- Staff of Rattosh: Aether, Vitality
- Stag: Pierce
- Typhos, the Jailor of Souls: Bleeding, Poison & Acid
- Ulo, the Keeper of the Waters: Elemental, Chaos, Poison & Acid
- Wolverine: Pierce, Vitality, Poison & Acid, Bleeding

New pet bonuses:

- Empty Throne: +4% Health for pets
- Rhowan's Crown: pet Elemental damage raised to 80% total (60% on one star becomes 40% on
  each of two stars), plus 80% pet Burn, Frostburn and Electrocute damage (40% per star)
- Wolverine: pet Defensive Ability raised to 6% total (2% and 4%), plus 5% Movement Speed for
  pets

Note: the patch notes list +5% Health for pets on Revenant. The game data has that bonus (on the
third star), but the star does not link to it, so it does not apply in game and the planner
does not show it. The same is true of an older pet life-leech bonus on the fourth star.

## Resistance Reduction

- Aura of Censure: -30% Elemental at rank 12 and -40% at max ultimate rank, up from -25% / -35%
- Rimetongue set, Blade Trap: -25% Cold and Pierce, up from -20%
- New: Word of Pain ascended modifier, -16% Aether, Chaos and Pierce
- New: Storm's Edge (Awakened), Aura of Censure -10% Lightning
- New: Bloodreaper's Cowl (Awakened), Devouring Swarm -10% Bleeding and -10% Pierce
- New: Awakened Bloodreaper's Butchery set, Devouring Swarm -15% Pierce
- New: Awakened Desecrator Covenant set, Ill Omen -18% Chaos
- New: Cleansed Blade of Elgoloth (Awakened), Storm Box of Elgoloth -8% Lightning
- New: Gutwrencher's Effigy, Thermite Mine -8% Chaos and -12% Physical
- Removed: Armageddon's flat -20% resistance on Cadence (removed by the patch as an error)

## Monster Resistances

- The Obsidian Swordsman (the new Kurn burial site) now has its own rows. Its empowered version
  has 80% to all resistances, and 90% Lightning and Vitality.
- Vengeful Spirit (the Frostveil altar encounter) is listed separately.
- New variants for Iglus Bloodmonger, Alluc the Thirster and the four rhino heroes. Their
  resistances are unchanged.

## Skill Items

- 12 new legendaries: the Obsidian Swordsman set (Branded Cage, Branded Guard, Branded Slayer,
  Branded Visage), the Igirron's Arsenal set (Igirron's Rupturer, Igirron's Sunderer, Igirron's
  Shards, Igirron's Visor), and Gutstompers, Gutwader Legwraps, Gutwrencher Grips and
  Gutwrencher's Effigy.
- The 29 newly Awakened epics, including the Bloodreaper's Butchery and Desecrator Covenant
  sets, now show as their Legendary versions.
- Item skill modifiers follow the patch, for example Stormrend's cooldown refreshes now sit on
  Feral Claws, and the Ultos' Tempest set's Primal Strike refresh is 1.5 seconds.
- Pet panels reflect the new pet base resistances (for example, Hellhound keeps its Fire
  immunity but loses its other resistances).
